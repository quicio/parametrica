## Why

The lattice network is the shared cellular model behind preview, fabrication
and perforation. Typing it stabilizes the last algorithmic geometry module
before the lattice solid kernel.

## What Changes

- Convert `geometry/latticeNetwork.js` to `.ts`.
- Type seeds, nodes, edges, cells, bands, curves and network stats.
- Keep the JS compatibility re-export.

## Out of Scope

- Lattice solid assembly and UI.
