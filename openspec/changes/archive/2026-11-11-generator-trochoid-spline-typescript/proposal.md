## Why

Trochoids and splines are the last classic 2D generator families.

## What Changes

- Convert `generators/trochoids.js` and `generators/splines.js` to `.ts`.
- Type trochoid/spirograph params, point helpers and sampling options.
- Type procedural spline nodes and Catmull/Bezier/Cubic params.
- Keep JS compatibility exports and update the generator index.

## Out of Scope

- Attractors, 3D/surface/lattice generators, operators, geometry and UI.
