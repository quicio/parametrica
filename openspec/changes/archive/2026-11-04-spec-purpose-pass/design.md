## Context

Purpose placeholders are generated by OpenSpec archive and never filled.

## Decisions

### D1: Purpose, not narrative

Each TBD Purpose becomes one to three sentences describing why the capability
exists. Requirements and scenarios are not rewritten.

### D2: Check

Add a simple check (Node script or test) that fails when any
`openspec/specs/**/spec.md` contains:

```text
TBD - created by archiving
```

### D3: Scope

The 24 existing specs in the audit are in scope. New specs are expected to
arrive with a purpose; the check enforces it after archive.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- Purpose statements can become stale; keep them conceptual, not numeric.
- The check is textual; it only catches the known placeholder.

## Open Questions

- Should `openspec validate` enforce non-empty Purpose natively?
