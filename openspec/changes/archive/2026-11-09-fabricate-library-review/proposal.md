## Why

The Fabricate category mixed ready objects with repair demonstrations and an
intentionally blocked topology challenge. A user browsing "Fabricate" expects
a body that can be exported and printed, not a diagnostic exercise.

## What Changes

- Audit every Fabricate preset with the real fabrication report.
- Remove the thin SDF demo and the intentionally blocked Möbius challenge.
- Add functional objects: Pencil Cup, Planter, Napkin Ring and Coaster.
- Add decorative/abstract objects: Bud Vase and Twisted Totem.
- Require every Fabricate preset to be exportable, have positive volume, bed
  contact and an overhang ratio below the warning threshold.

## Out of Scope

- New generators or fabrication algorithms.
- Changing the existing vase/lattice geometry.
- Slicer verification automation.
