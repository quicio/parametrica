## Types

- `Point2DLike`, `Point3DLike`, `Bounds3DLike`, `Box3DLike`.
- `TriangleIntersection` discriminated union for segment/point/coplanar.
- Typed BVH nodes and intersection pair results.

## Rules

- Exact BigInt determinants and EPS bounds are unchanged.
- BVH split order, pair limits and tolerance defaults are unchanged.
