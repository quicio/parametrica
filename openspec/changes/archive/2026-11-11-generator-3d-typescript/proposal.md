## Why

The 3D curve and surface generators close the remaining spatial generator
families before attractors/lattice and operators.

## What Changes

- Convert `parametric3D`, `helix` and `parametricSurface` to `.ts`.
- Type 3D/surface params, generation and projection boundaries.
- Keep JS compatibility exports and update the generator index.

## Out of Scope

- Attractors, surface lattice, operators, geometry and UI.
