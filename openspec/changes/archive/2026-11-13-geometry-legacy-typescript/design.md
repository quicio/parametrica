## Types

- `LegacyPoint`/`LegacyCurve` bridge shapes to/from canonical curves.
- `Vec2`, `BezierSegment` and Catmull-Rom options.
- `GlyphParams`, `GlyphMetrics` and `SemanticPoint` roles.

## Rules

- Knot math, tangents, sampling and optical offsets are unchanged.
- Mirrored geometry remains generated only by reflection.
