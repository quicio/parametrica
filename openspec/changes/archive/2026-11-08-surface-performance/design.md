## Benchmark matrix

- Surface grids at three sample densities.
- Quad and single view.
- Picking at fixed screen points.
- Seam overlay on/off.
- Playback for a fixed number of frames.

## Budgets (provisional)

- Scene build, pick, overlay and playback recorded, not guaranteed.
- Degrade overlays or preview detail before blocking interaction.
- Report the measured bottleneck.

## Acceptance

- Benchmark script is reproducible.
- Dense scene remains interactive or reports degradation.
- No geometry/export change.
