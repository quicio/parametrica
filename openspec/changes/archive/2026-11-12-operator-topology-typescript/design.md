## Types

- Shared `Vec2`/`CurveLike` shapes and a typed array transform helper.
- `radialArray` params are typed through `Rec` and coerced explicitly.
- Delaunay exposes `triangles`, `halfedges`, `points` and `voronoi`.

## Rules

- Array distributions, sweep math and ids are unchanged.
- Skeleton pruning, point-in-polygon and voronoi cell filters are unchanged.
