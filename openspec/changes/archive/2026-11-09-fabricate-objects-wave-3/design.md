## Lampshade Ring Set

- Three concentric shells: outer 170 mm/180 mm, middle 130 mm/150 mm, inner
  90 mm/120 mm.
- Each shell is an outer−inner difference with a 2 mm wall.
- Independent groups export three bodies.
- All three sit on the bed with disjoint annular footprints.

## Stackable Tray 160

- Rounded superellipse shell, 160 × 90 mm at the base, 3 mm wall.
- The top 22% of the height widens by 4 mm to form a locating lip.
- The bottom footprint fits inside the lip with 2 mm clearance.
- One difference, no groups.

## Acceptance

- The ring set reports three bodies and three groups.
- The tray is exportable with positive bed contact and overhang below 20%.
- Product preview works for the grouped mesh.
