## Why

The second wave covered single-body objects. A lampshade set needs independent
hollow shades, and a stackable tray needs a top locating lip without a second
boolean.

## What Changes

- Add `Lampshade Ring Set`: three concentric open shades as separate bodies.
- Add `Stackable Tray 160`: a rounded tray with a 4 mm top lip.
- Extend the rounded-shell builder with expressions in v.
- Add group metadata to the Fabricate report and UI.

## Out of Scope

- A folding hinge or sequential CSG.
- Interlocking modular tiles.
